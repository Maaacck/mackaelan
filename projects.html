Robotics · May 2025 – Oct 2025

Bioinspired Hexapod Robot

A six-legged walking robot modeled on how insects move. I took it from CAD model to printed parts to working code between May and October 2025.

Design

  • Designed the body and all six legs in Fusion 360.
  • 3D-printed every structural part.

Electronics & code

  • Programmed the robot in C++.
  • Used a Teensy microcontroller together with a Raspberry Pi.

Built with

  • C++
  • Teensy
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Fusion 360
  • 3D printing

Audio hardware · Aug 2026 – Sep 2026

Custom Headphones

A custom pair of headphones I designed and assembled myself in August and September 2026.

Design & printing

  • Modeled the case and clips in Fusion 360.
  • Printed the parts in PLA, ABS, and PETG.

Electronics

  • Soldered the drivers to 0.78 mm 2-pin connectors, a common standard for detachable headphone cables.

Built with

  • Fusion 360
  • 3D printing
  • Soldering
